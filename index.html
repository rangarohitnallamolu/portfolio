ABOUT ME

I've been drawn to data for as long as I can remember — the challenge of turning messy, disconnected information into something a business can actually act on. That curiosity is what pulled me from analytics into data engineering, and more recently into building agentic AI systems that can reason over data on their own.

As a Data Engineer with 3+ years of experience across healthcare, finance, and education, I build scalable batch and real-time pipelines on Databricks, Apache Spark, and Azure, using Delta Lake and Medallion Architecture, and I connect them to SAP Datasphere and SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) for financial planning, forecasting, and analytics. My focus is reliable data: automated quality checks, governance, and Spark optimization. Outside of client work, I built StockSense, an AI-driven stock trading and research platform powered by multi-agent workflows (Claude, LangGraph) where Bull and Bear agents debate each trade and automated risk and stop-loss checks guard execution — a project born from wanting to go deeper into agentic AI and RAG.

Outside of work, I play cricket for fun and compete as a badminton player — recent highlights include the Hoyasola Super League 2024 Championship and podium finishes at the Frisco Open and Nexus Open in 2025. Competitive sports have taught me the same discipline and focus I bring to solving hard data problems.
